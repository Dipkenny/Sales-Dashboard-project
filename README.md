# Pizza Sales Analysis

## Business Problem
Management is interested in identifying opportunities within the dataset that can help drive more sales and improve operational efficiency. 

## Tools Used
-Power BI
-Power Query 
-DAX

## Data Set
Online Pizza Data set

## Key Insights
-Friday recorded the highest monthly revenue.
-July recorded the highest monthly revenue
-September and October recorded the lowest monthly revenue
-Large-size pizzas contributed the highest share of revenue
-Big meat s was the best selling pizza
-The Greek XXL was among the least selling pizza

## Recommendations
-Promote high-performing pizzas and categories to maximize revenue
-Investigate low-performing pizzas to understand why demand is lower
-Plan inventory and staffing around peak ordering periods
-Use customer ordering patterns to create targeted promotions and combo offers
-Monitor sales trends regularly through the Power BI dashboard
